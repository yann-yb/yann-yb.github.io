# Test page

This is a sample memo for trying out the reading layout. A small place for thoughts, questions, and things worth coming back to.

Open [Linked note](#linked-note) or [Another thought](#another-thought) to try the panels.

An idea does not need to be finished before it is written down. Sometimes a short note is enough to remember what felt interesting.

> Keep the thought small enough to explore, and clear enough to revisit.

A few things to try:

- Capture a question.
- Add an observation.
- Leave room for the next thought.

A note can also hold a little code:

```text
observe → question → explore
```

This is only a test page. Replace it with your own memo whenever you like.
